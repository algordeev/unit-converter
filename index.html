How it works
    Syntax & supported units

    Write quantities explicitly: 5 ft + 20 cm; 12 N*m * 1500 rpm. Use * or × between quantities and / or ÷ for division. Unit products also accept ·. No functions, variables, or implicit quantity multiplication.

    Precedence: unit powers first, then an attached unit product (12 kg*m/s² is one quantity), quantity powers, unary signs, multiplication/division left to right, then addition/subtraction. Parentheses override order. Write 10 m / (2 s * 3 s) for a grouped denominator. Signed numbers are supported. Parenthesize a whole quantity to raise it: (2 m)^2; 2 m² means 2 square meters. For example, −2^2 = −4 and (−2)^2 = 4. Use integer powers from −12 to 12; m² and m³ also work.

    Numbers: decimal point only; no commas or thousands separators. Scientific notation: 1.5e3 W. Results use up to 10 significant digits and scientific notation below 1e−6 or at/above 1e10. ≈ marks rounded display values, not measurement accuracy. Calculations retain browser number precision. Limits: 512 characters per field, 160 tokens and 24 nesting levels.

    Output: append “in km/h” or “to km/h”, or use the optional field. Supplying both is an error; clear one. Without a target, results use base SI units, without guessing whether N*m means torque or energy. Equal dimensions can cancel to a dimensionless result.

    Temperature: direct absolute conversions only, e.g. −40 °C in °F. All arithmetic with °C, °F or K is rejected; use deltaC, deltaF or deltaK for temperature differences. Pressure units represent magnitudes; no gauge/absolute offset is inferred. rpm includes 2π radians per revolution.

    Case matters: mW and MW differ. Use gal_US / gal_imp and tonne / ton_short / ton_long. cal and kcal are thermochemical; BTU is the international-table variant.

    Supported symbols: .

    Aliases (case-sensitive): . Use symbols for other units. Unit-only targets accept products, division and integer powers; target parentheses and numeric scale factors are unsupported.
